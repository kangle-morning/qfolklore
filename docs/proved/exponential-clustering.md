## Intuition

A gapped quantum many-body ground state has only **short-range correlations**.

Very roughly, if $A_X$ and $B_Y$ are local observables supported far away from each other, then the connected correlation
$$
\omega(A_X B_Y)-\omega(A_X)\omega(B_Y)
$$
becomes exponentially small as the distance between $X$ and $Y$ increases.

The conceptual reason is that a spectral gap together with sufficiently local dynamics prevents generic long-range connected correlations in the ground state.

---

## Statement

Let $\Gamma$ be a countable metric lattice, and let $\Phi$ be an interaction on $\Gamma$ such that the corresponding dynamics satisfies a Lieb--Robinson bound. For example, $\Phi$ may be finite-range or exponentially decaying. Let $\omega$ be an infinite-volume ground state.

Assume that in the GNS representation $(\mathcal H_\omega,\pi_\omega,\Omega_\omega)$ of $\omega$, the dynamics is generated by a nonnegative Hamiltonian $H_\omega$, and that the ground-state sector is separated from the rest of the spectrum by a positive gap $\Delta>0$. That is,
$$
\sigma(H_\omega)\setminus \{0\} \subset [\Delta,\infty).
$$

Then there exist constants $C,\mu>0$ such that for any bounded local observables $A_X,B_Y$ supported on finite sets $X,Y\subset \Gamma$,
$$
\left|
\omega(A_X B_Y)-\omega(A_X)\omega(B_Y)
\right|
\le
C \, \|A_X\|\,\|B_Y\|\, \min(|X|,|Y|)\, e^{-\mu\, \mathrm{dist}(X,Y)} .
$$

More generally, one often writes a variant with a different harmless support-size prefactor, for example
$$
\left|
\omega(A_X B_Y)-\omega(A_X)\omega(B_Y)
\right|
\le
C' \, \|A_X\|\,\|B_Y\|\, |X|\,|Y|\, e^{-\mu\, \mathrm{dist}(X,Y)} .
$$

In the case of a degenerate gapped ground-state sector, a standard sharper formulation says that if $P_0$ denotes the projection onto the ground-state subspace and $B_Y$ has no matrix elements inside that subspace, for example if
$$
P_0\,\pi_\omega(B_Y)\,P_0 = 0,
$$
then one has the bound
$$
|\omega(A_X B_Y)|
\le
C \,\|A_X\|\,\|B_Y\|\, e^{-\mu\,\mathrm{dist}(X,Y)},
$$
up to the same type of support-size prefactors.

---

## Assumptions

Typical sufficient hypotheses are:

- the interaction is sufficiently local for a Lieb--Robinson bound to hold;
- the observables are bounded and finitely supported;
- the ground state exists;
- there is a nonzero spectral gap above the ground-state sector.

Depending on the formulation, one may assume either a unique ground state or a finite-dimensional gapped ground-state sector.

**Proved.**
